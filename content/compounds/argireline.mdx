## What it is

Argireline is a six amino acid peptide modelled on the N-terminal end of SNAP-25, a protein in the SNARE complex that vesicles use to release acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction. Botulinum toxin cleaves SNAP-25. Argireline is supposed to compete with it, destabilising the complex and reducing muscle contraction.

Hence the marketing: topical Botox, Botox in a jar, needle-free.

## Why the comparison cannot be right

Botulinum toxin is injected into the muscle. Argireline is applied to the skin surface.

For a topical peptide to reduce muscle contraction it would have to cross the stratum corneum, traverse the epidermis and dermis, and reach the neuromuscular junctions of the underlying facial muscles in a concentration sufficient to compete with an abundant structural protein. Peptides of this size cross intact skin poorly. The claim requires delivery that has never been demonstrated.

The safety profile is quiet for the same reason the mechanism is doubtful: almost nothing reaches anywhere.

## What the trials actually found

Modest, real improvements that are probably not the advertised mechanism.

A double-blind randomised trial compared acetylhexapeptide-3 cream with palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 cream, the active in [Matrixyl](/compounds/matrixyl), for crow's feet, and found measurable improvement. More recent work on a serum containing acetyl hexapeptide-8 reported changes in skin biomarkers, wrinkle measures and texture in ex vivo and clinical evaluation.

The likeliest explanation is not neuromuscular. Well-formulated peptide serums hydrate, and hydration improves the appearance of fine lines within hours. Some peptides also have signalling effects in the dermis on matrix production. Either accounts for a modest result without anything reaching a muscle.

## Practical notes

- **Judge it as a decent peptide serum, not as an injectable substitute.** On that basis it is inexpensive, well tolerated and does something small.
- **Percentage on the label is ambiguous.** Products list the concentration of the trade solution, which is itself mostly water, so a stated 10% is not 10% peptide.
- **Nothing topical substitutes for a neuromodulator.** If the goal is reducing dynamic wrinkles from muscle movement, this is the wrong category rather than a weaker option in the right one.
