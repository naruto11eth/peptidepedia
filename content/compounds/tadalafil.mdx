## What it is

Tadalafil inhibits phosphodiesterase type 5, the enzyme that degrades cGMP in vascular smooth muscle. Blocking it means cGMP persists, smooth muscle stays relaxed, and blood flow increases - in the penis, and to a lesser extent in the prostate, lungs and systemic vasculature.

It is one of only two compounds in this reference that is both **approved and cheap**, which is worth stating plainly given how much of this site documents unapproved material.

## Why the half-life changes the strategy

Sildenafil lasts around 4 hours. Tadalafil lasts around 17.5 - roughly a day and a half of coverage from a single dose, which is where the "weekend pill" nickname came from.

That duration enables **low-dose daily** use, and daily use is a different intervention from as-needed use:

- Effect becomes continuous rather than scheduled, removing the timing problem entirely.
- It is the dosing pattern approved for **BPH** - daily tadalafil improves lower urinary tract symptoms.
- Sustained PDE5 inhibition is the basis of the endothelial-function arguments that make it a longevity-community staple. Those effects are real in mechanistic terms; the hard-outcome evidence is thinner than the enthusiasm.

## Practical notes

- **Nitrates are an absolute contraindication.** Not a caution. The combination causes profound hypotension, and this includes recreational nitrite poppers, which is the version that actually sends people to emergency rooms.
- **Alpha-blockers** for blood pressure or prostate need dose separation and medical supervision.
- **PDE5 inhibitors do not create desire.** They address the vascular step. Low libido with normal erectile function is a different problem, usually hormonal - which is where [enclomiphene](/compounds/enclomiphene) enters the same conversation.
- **Do not stack PDE5 inhibitors.** Tadalafil plus [vardenafil](/compounds/vardenafil) compounds hypotension without adding benefit.
- **New erectile dysfunction is a cardiovascular signal.** The penile arteries are small and narrow first. Treating the symptom without investigating the cause misses the more important finding.
