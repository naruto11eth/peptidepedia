## What it is

Vardenafil is a PDE5 inhibitor with the same core mechanism as [tadalafil](/compounds/tadalafil) and sildenafil, differing in selectivity and duration.

## How the three compare

| | Sildenafil | Vardenafil | Tadalafil |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Half-life | ~4 h | ~4-5 h | ~17.5 h |
| Onset | 30-60 min | 30-60 min | 30-120 min |
| Food interaction | Significant - fat delays it | Moderate | Minimal |
| Daily dosing viable | No | No | Yes |
| Distinctive side effect | Blue-tinted vision (PDE6 cross-reactivity) | QT prolongation signal | Back and muscle ache |

Vardenafil's selectivity for PDE5 over PDE6 - the retinal enzyme - is higher than sildenafil's, which is why the blue-vision effect is much less common. That selectivity is its main argument.

## Practical notes

- **This is a situational drug.** If you want continuous coverage, tadalafil's half-life is the reason it exists; vardenafil cannot do that job.
- **Food matters less than with sildenafil but is not irrelevant** - a heavy fatty meal still delays onset.
- **The QT signal is the reason to check your other medications.** Combined with other QT-prolonging drugs, or in known long QT syndrome, another agent is the better choice.
- **Nitrates, absolutely not.** Same as the rest of the class.
